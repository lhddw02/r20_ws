On Jetson Ubuntu 22.04 / ROS Humble:
1. unzip this archive into ~/r20_ws
2. bash ~/r20_ws/src/snail_bottle_approach/scripts/setup_jetson.sh ~/r20_ws
3. Follow ~/r20_ws/src/snail_bottle_approach/README.md; first test enable_motion:=false.
Default stop distance: 0.40 m from the forward-facing camera.
The model detects generic bottles; validate real depth on your bottle before driving.
